- **2026-10-06 colour steps between columns, then the tile lattice.** The 16 × 53 scan `260128` was interrupted at point 755 (`Y movement failed: rc=-1`) and finished in a second camera session (gain 27 vs 21); the mosaic showed a bright strip over the last two columns. A first version of `tools/colour_match_grid.py` corrected one gain per column from the registered column overlaps; the strip disappeared but a faint lattice at the tile pitch (~1 %) remained, from tile-to-tile exposure differences and the ~1 % error of the stitcher's render-time flat field. The tool now (1) estimates the illumination field from a few hundred tiles (per-pixel median of mean-normalised tiles, smoothed with sigma = width/32; a cubic polynomial was tried and flattened the vertical tilt by half, so do not reintroduce it), (2) measures every horizontal and vertical neighbour pair, (3) solves one level per column plus a penalised per-tile deviation (`TILE_PENALTY` 0.3; edge weights = flat share of the overlap, Huber 3 %) after removing the median-of-boundary-medians of each edge kind. Two lessons from the real data are built into that design: an unpenalised per-tile solve chained a 0.1 %/row bias of the vertical overlaps into ±10 % ramps along columns (the bare-substrate plateau along those columns is flat to ±1 %, so the ramps were artefacts), and the overlaps inside the bright crystal and at the chip edge gave gains of ±5–17 % for single tiles that their substrate does not support. `tests/test_colour_match_grid.py` (12) covers field recovery, per-tile jitter plus a resumed-run step, column mode, mirroring (scanner `invert_x` moves left, the stitcher wants columns increasing to the right), hard links / byte copies, derived records accepted by the stitcher's acquisition check, `--report-only`, `--full` and the plausibility limit. Known: `run_stitch.py`'s closed-form focus gate is content-biased on single-shot flat grids (sparse bare-substrate tiles score low and are first marked defocus; `step_rescue` keeps them because nothing else covers their footprint). Next: wire the tool into the workbench stitch-setup page; on the scanner side, implement the motion-failure policy sketched in `docs/diagnostics/stage_error_incident_log_zh.md` in AmScope-Camera (read back counters before deciding to stop, retry a lost `command_movr` once, never soft-stop first).
